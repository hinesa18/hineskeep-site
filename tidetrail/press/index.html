Press kit

Tide Trail is a calm one-line logic puzzle for iPhone. Draw one line through every square of a tide pool, passing the numbered shells in order, and watch the litter wash away; when the pool is clean, Pebble the otter swims your route.

One line. Every tile. Can you clean the whole pool?

What's in at launch

  • 1,000 levels, each checked by a solver to have exactly one solution, with reef walls, one-way currents and, from level 45, kelp arches the line crosses twice.
  • Stars and chests: up to three stars on every level, a star chest every 30 stars and a level chest every five pools.
  • Daily gifts and weekly goals: a 7-day gift calendar that waits for you if you miss a day, and three new goals every Monday.
  • Make it yours: any critter from your album can swim your route, with eight trail colors, a rainbow trail and four pool themes (Coral Reef, Night Tide, Arctic and Sunset). Every look can be earned with coins; the Collector's Pack unlocks them all at once.
  • The Daily Tide: one untimed puzzle a day, the same for everyone, with streak rewards, earned streak freezes for busy days, a Game Center leaderboard for the day's fastest solve, and a spoiler-free card to share your time and streak.
  • The Winter Pool Clean-Up (December 12 to January 10): clean 30 pools to win the icy Frost trail.
  • Calm by design: normal levels have no timer and can't be lost, every puzzle plays offline with one finger, and ads appear only after a win: never mid-puzzle, never before level 12 and never after a Daily Tide.

About the game

The one-line path puzzle is a classic logic format, but on the App Store it exists only as plain, abstract apps or as a mode inside bigger puzzle collections. Tide Trail gives it a warm, original world: illustrated sea critters, gentle haptics and sounds, and a dirty-to-clean payoff on every level. Bottle caps and scraps of net litter the sand; as your stream passes, each square turns to clear turquoise water.

It's built to be a daily habit for adults who unwind with a brain game: one puzzle a day, a streak worth keeping, and a pool that's always a little cleaner than you left it.
