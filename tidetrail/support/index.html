Tide Trail support

Tide Trail is in development for iPhone. For questions, feedback or help with a test build, email support@hineskeep.com. A real person (Austin, the developer) reads every message.

How do I play?

Start on shell 1 and drag your finger up, down, left or right. Pass the numbered shells in order (1, 2, 3…), visit every square of the tide pool exactly once, and finish on the last shell. Every level has exactly one solution.

I reached the last shell, but the level didn't finish

Every square must be filled. If you reach the last shell with squares still empty, a coral message says "Fill every square!" and the empty squares pulse. Drag back along your line to undo, then route through the squares you missed.

How do the blue arrow squares work?

From level 21, blue arrow squares are ocean currents. Your line can cross a current square only in the direction its arrow points: you can't enter it against the arrow or turn while you're on it.

How do I undo?

Drag back along your line as far as you like, or tap Undo to go back to the previous shell. Wrong moves never cost anything.

What are Hard levels and the tide?

Hard and Super Hard levels have a tide timer. If the tide goes out before you finish, you can keep your line and get more time, or simply retry. Freeze stops the tide for 20 seconds.

Is my progress saved? What if I delete the app?

Your progress is saved automatically on your iPhone. There's no account, so deleting the app also deletes your progress, coins and hints.

Can I buy anything?

Not yet: this version has no in-app purchases and no ads. This page will explain purchases and how to restore them before any are added.

Found a bug?

Email support@hineskeep.com and include:

  1. your iPhone model and iOS version (for example, iPhone 16, iOS 26);
  2. the level number you were on;
  3. what happened, and a screenshot if you can.
