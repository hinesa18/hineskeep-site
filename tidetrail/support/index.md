# Tide Trail support

**Tide Trail is in development for iPhone.** For questions, feedback, or help with a test build, contact Hines Keep, the independent studio of Austin Hines.

- **Support Email:** support@hineskeep.com
- **Release status:** In development for iOS

## FAQ for the planned v1.0 release

These instructions describe planned release features and may change. Test builds may not yet include every setting or purchase option.

### 1. How do I restore my "Remove Ads" or "Starter Pack" purchase on a new iPhone?
1. Open **Tide Trail** and tap the **Settings gear icon** on the Home screen.
2. Tap **"Restore Purchases"**.
3. Make sure you are signed into the same Apple ID you used when buying the item. Your **Remove Ads** status (`org.austinhines.tidetrail.remove_ads`) or **Starter Pack** (`org.austinhines.tidetrail.starter_pack`) will be restored immediately at no charge! *(Note: Consumable coin packs are saved locally to your device when purchased).*

### 2. Why didn't the level finish when my line reached the last numbered shell?
In **Tide Trail**, your stream must **pass the numbered shells in order (1, 2, 3...) AND visit every single square of the tide pool** so all the sand is washed clean! If you reach the final shell while any squares are still empty, a coral message chip (`"Fill every square! N are still empty"`) will appear and the empty squares will pulse. Simply drag your finger backward along your line to undo and route through the remaining squares!

### 3. How do the blue arrow squares ("Currents") work from Level 21 onward?
Blue arrow squares are **Ocean Currents**. Your line may only cross a Current square **straight in the direction the arrow points** (you cannot enter against the arrow or turn 90 degrees while on top of a Current square).

### 4. How do I turn off Sound, Music, Haptics, or the Daily Tide Reminder?
- **Sound, Music & Haptics:** Tap the **Settings gear icon** on the Home screen inside Tide Trail and toggle **Sound**, **Music**, or **Haptics** on or off.
- **Daily Reminder Notification:** Open your iPhone's **Settings → Notifications → Tide Trail** and toggle **Allow Notifications** off.

### 5. Is my progress saved if I delete the app?
In v1.0, your game progress is stored locally on your iPhone (`player_data.json`) to protect your privacy and allow offline puzzle play without an account. Deleting the app will reset your local level progress and coins, though any non-consumable purchases (*Remove Ads* / *Starter Pack*) can always be recovered via **Restore Purchases**.

## Still Need Help or Found a Bug?
Email us at **support@hineskeep.com** and let us know:
1. Your iPhone model and iOS version (e.g., iPhone 16, iOS 18.2);
2. Which level number you are on; and
3. A short description (or screenshot) of what happened.

The release privacy policy will be published before launch. For privacy questions, email [support@hineskeep.com](mailto:support@hineskeep.com).
