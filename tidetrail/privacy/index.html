Tide Trail privacy policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026. This policy covers the current pre-release version of Tide Trail for iPhone, made by Hines Keep (Austin Hines, an independent developer in Michigan, USA). It also covers this website.

The short version

  • Tide Trail does not collect personal data. Hines Keep receives nothing about you or your device from the game.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases in this version.
  • Your progress is saved in one file on your iPhone and never leaves it, except in your own iPhone backups.
  • The game works offline. You don't need an account.

What the game stores on your iPhone

Tide Trail keeps one save file inside the app's private storage on your iPhone. It holds:

  • your progress: the next level, how many levels you have finished, and how many of them were Hard;
  • your coins, hints and freezes, and whether you've received the free hint and freeze gifts;
  • which sea critters you have unlocked and which one swims your wins;
  • daily puzzle streak counters (for a feature that isn't in the game yet);
  • your sound, music and vibration settings;
  • on/off flags for future purchases (always off in this version, because nothing is for sale);
  • the time of your first launch and of the last save, how many times you've opened the game, and the level at which the game asked iOS to show the rating prompt.

None of this identifies you. The file isn't sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, iOS may include the file in that backup, which belongs to you and which we can't access.

No network, no ads, no analytics

The current version of Tide Trail doesn't contain any advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or in-app purchase software, and its own code doesn't connect to the internet. The "test ad" card you may see in a test build is a placeholder inside the game, and no ad is loaded from anywhere.

Tide Trail is made with the Unity game engine. We haven't turned on any of Unity's online services (such as Unity Analytics, Unity Ads or Unity's crash reporting), and we receive no data from Unity about you. Unity describes the technical information its software can handle in its player privacy policy.

Rating prompt

Once, after you win a level from level 15 on, Tide Trail asks iOS to show Apple's standard "Enjoying Tide Trail?" rating prompt. iOS decides whether it appears. Any star rating or review you leave goes to Apple under Apple's privacy policy. The game never sees it.

Notifications, location, camera, contacts

This version sends no notifications and doesn't use your location, camera, microphone, photos or contacts.

Children

Tide Trail is a puzzle game for a general audience. It doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including children.

When you email us

If you write to support@hineskeep.com, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to send. We use it only to answer you, and we never sell or share it. Mail to that address is forwarded by Cloudflare's email routing to the developer's Google Gmail inbox. To have your messages deleted, just ask at the same address.

This website

hineskeep.com uses no cookies, analytics, ads or third-party scripts. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, and our domain is registered with Cloudflare. Like any web host, these services handle visitors' IP addresses to deliver pages and protect against abuse. See GitHub's privacy statement and Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

Before Tide Trail adds ads, analytics, in-app purchases or reminders, we'll update this policy to explain exactly what changes, and we'll change the date at the top. The App Store listing's privacy details will be updated at the same time.
